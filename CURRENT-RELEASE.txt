Pool Shed v1.47.2 - iPad navigation and tablet layout

The sidebar now becomes a hideable menu at iPad widths, including landscape and larger touch tablets. Selecting a section or subsection closes it; tapping outside or pressing Escape does the same. Navigation targets are larger for touch. The audit workspace gains the full tablet canvas while the existing customer and finance workflows remain intact.

Pool Shed v1.47.1 - supplier payment operations and Sales Order costs

Finance Command now separates unpaid proforma Purchase Orders from on-account payables. Proforma orders are due in full before supplier release and require an actual supplier payment with a reference; they do not enter the payment run. On-account bills enter a payment run only after the PO, receipt and invoice match, and an outstanding balance remains.

Rach can choose a due-date range, include already overdue bills, run a review, select items, create a saved draft payment batch, inspect and export it, or cancel the draft. Creating a batch never records a bank payment. The PO now records supplier invoice and payment due dates. A clear Net N days term can calculate a due date from the invoice date; an explicit due date takes precedence. Bills with no due date are called out for review.

Each PO line can link to a non-Project Sales Order. Finance Command shows indicative Sales Order gross profit from linked PO costs and flags incomplete coverage. This is an operational estimate, not a final ledger profit calculation. Projects retain their own reporting.

Shared workspace saving, audit links and existing records remain intact. Xero remains the accounting ledger and any PO/Xero balance discrepancy is marked for reconciliation.

Live follow-up: when a Sales Order has incomplete PO cost coverage, the profit and margin cells remain Pending rather than displaying a misleading 100% margin.
