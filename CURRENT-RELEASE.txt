Pool Shed v1.46.1 - CRM-linked commercial audit organisations

Organisation Name in Pool Audits selects an existing CRM customer profile. The selected customer ID determines which profile owns the audit, and the CRM company name is used in the saved report.

An empty draft can be linked to another customer profile from the survey. Once findings, assets, actions or evidence have been captured, its customer link is locked to protect the audit history and customer-owned media. Start a new audit for another customer instead.

Existing customer and audit records remain intact. No database migration or data reset is required.
