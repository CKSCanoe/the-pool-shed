Pool Shed v1.54.0 - Sales invoicing in the Pool Shed ledger

Accounting → Invoice Ready lists every delivered order with its exact total, worked out from the order just as the Sales screens show it. Review the customer, lines and total, tick to confirm and post: the invoice gets the next number (carrying on from INV-2048) and goes into the ledger. Orders not yet delivered can have a deposit invoice, which the final invoice deducts automatically; projects billed by stage get stage invoices that can never exceed the accepted quote plus approved extras. Orders invoiced before the ledger are listed but can never be invoiced twice. Invoices are due on receipt unless the customer has their own terms. Each invoice or credit note downloads as a professional PDF, and can be emailed with the PDF attached once invoice emails are switched on (off for now) and the invoice details are complete (VAT number and bank details). Corrections are credit notes with a reason (returns, price corrections); customer payments are recorded once and applied to invoices, with anything extra kept on account to apply later or refund. Credit Control shows aged debtors. Unpaid invoices already in Xero can be loaded as opening balances at go-live. The order screen's Invoice button now opens Invoice Ready, so numbers are only ever issued by the ledger.

Pool Shed v1.53.0 - Monthly van report reminders

Engineers now see a reminder at the top of Pool Shed, and a count on Vans & Fleet in the menu, until this month's report for their van is submitted; if a report is sent back, the reminder shows the reviewer's comment. Admin and Management see how many reports are waiting for their sign-off and how many vans have not reported yet. The reminder can be hidden for the rest of the day. Optional reminder emails, which stay switched off until the owner turns them on: engineers on the 1st of the month and then every 3 days until they submit (and straight away if a report is sent back); Admin and Management on the 8th, 15th and 22nd with every van still outstanding. No one is emailed twice on the same day.

Pool Shed v1.52.0 - Vans & Fleet

New Vans & Fleet section. Add each van with its registration plate, make, model, year, colour, fuel, VIN, mileage and assigned engineer; MOT, road tax, insurance (insurer, policy, renewal), service and breakdown cover dates with due-soon and overdue warnings; V5, insurance and MOT documents; and the supplies that should be on board. Pool Shed v1.51.0 - Record a purchase and Payment runs (Pool Shed ledger)

Accounts can record anything already bought in one step (supplier, invoice or receipt number, job or cost category, net and VAT, a photo or PDF of the invoice). Pro-forma suppliers are paid up front: the purchase is only complete with its payment recorded. On-account suppliers are due on their terms and paid through payment runs: due, approved bills with their invoice copy; created by one person, approved by another, then paid, which records each supplier payment and settles the bills. Duplicate supplier invoice numbers are refused, larger purchases need approval by a different person, and corrections are credit notes, never edits. Supplier invoices and receipts are stored privately for Admin, Management and Accounts only.

Pool Shed v1.50.0 - role-based visibility

Each role now receives only what it may see, enforced by the database: Warehouse and Engineer accounts never receive costs or margins; only Admin, Management and Accounts receive payments and finance; Engineers see only the jobs they are assigned to (new "Assigned engineers" field on the job form) and those jobs' orders, without prices. A restricted user's saves can never erase hidden costs, payments or records. Azzy, the project AI review and Quote Studio follow the same rules. Admin and Management are unchanged.

Pool Shed v1.49.0 - fast per-record saving and refresh

Saving now sends only the records you changed (typically 1-3 KB) instead of the whole 7 MB workspace, so saves are several times faster and no longer hit the server time limit. When two people edit different records at the same time, both changes are kept; only two people changing the same record get a clash. Other open tabs download only the records that changed. If anything does not line up, the app falls back to the full save or download it used before. Also fixes false "Another user saved first" messages after two quick edits.

When someone saves, other open tabs now download only the records that changed (typically 1-10 KB) instead of the whole 7 MB workspace. If anything does not line up, the tab downloads in full exactly as before. Also fixes false "Another user saved first" messages after two quick edits by the same person: the saved revision stamp now matches the one returned to the app.

Pool Shed v1.47.1 - supplier payment operations and Sales Order costs

Finance Command now separates unpaid proforma Purchase Orders from on-account payables. Proforma orders are due in full before supplier release and require an actual supplier payment with a reference; they do not enter the payment run. On-account bills enter a payment run only after the PO, receipt and invoice match, and an outstanding balance remains.

Rach can choose a due-date range, include already overdue bills, run a review, select items, create a saved draft payment batch, inspect and export it, or cancel the draft. Creating a batch never records a bank payment. The PO now records supplier invoice and payment due dates. A clear Net N days term can calculate a due date from the invoice date; an explicit due date takes precedence. Bills with no due date are called out for review.

Each PO line can link to a non-Project Sales Order. Finance Command shows indicative Sales Order gross profit from linked PO costs and flags incomplete coverage. This is an operational estimate, not a final ledger profit calculation. Projects retain their own reporting.

Shared workspace saving, audit links and existing records remain intact. Xero remains the accounting ledger and any PO/Xero balance discrepancy is marked for reconciliation.

Live follow-up: when a Sales Order has incomplete PO cost coverage, the profit and margin cells remain Pending rather than displaying a misleading 100% margin.
