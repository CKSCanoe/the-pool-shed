Pool Shed v1.63.0 - Contracts: recurring maintenance and repeat orders

Sales → Subscriptions is now Sales → Contracts. A contract is either maintenance (charged as a fixed fee per period, in advance or in arrears, or per visit) or a repeat order of products; it can mix service lines and products. It runs weekly, every 2 or 4 weeks, monthly, quarterly, every 6 months or yearly, following the calendar. Contract prices are the agreed prices, so customer discounts are not applied. Contracts that are due are listed, on the page and on Home, and one click creates an order for each ticked contract to check; nothing is created, sent or invoiced on its own. The same period can never be created twice, a contract with an end date stops after it, and renewals due in the next 60 days are listed with an optional price change. The page shows active contracts and regular income a month and a year. Existing subscriptions keep working.
