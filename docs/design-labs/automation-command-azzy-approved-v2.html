Automation Health

Trigger → decision → action → proof
1 · Trigger
Something changes

Invoice becomes overdue, PO becomes late, stock crosses threshold or a record is created.

2 · Conditions
Check context

Amount, customer status, supplier promise, allocation, project and permissions.

3 · Decision
Rule + Azzy

Deterministic rules first. AI only where judgement or summarisation adds value.

4 · Guardrail
Authority check

Automatic, approval-required or suggest-only based on risk and user permissions.

5 · Action
Do useful work

Create task, prepare chase, draft PO, flag account, route exception or notify staff.

6 · Proof
Audit everything

Source record, reason, user/automation, before/after state and outcome retained.
